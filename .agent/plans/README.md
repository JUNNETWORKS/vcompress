# Plans

ExecPlans for this repository. The format and requirements are defined in
`.agent/PLANS.md`; `TEMPLATE.md` in this directory is the starting point.

Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`, dated by when the plan was started.

Plans are committed. They are the record of why non-obvious changes were made,
what was tried, and what surprised us — keep them updated while the work is in
flight, and finish them with an Outcomes and retrospective section rather than
deleting them.
